About Myesha

For over two decades, Myesha Starks has been shaping the looks that bring characters to life on screen. An Emmy-nominated Department Head and Key Hairstylist, she has led departments and worked hands-on as personal hairstylist to A-list talent including Vince Vaughn and Aunjanue Ellis Taylor.

From natural hair and wig work to period and character design, Myesha brings precision, continuity, and an instinct for what a story needs to every chair. Her extensive television & film credits include:

  • Love, Victor
  • Lucky
  • Bad Monkey
  • 9-1-1
  • Sweetwater
  • Genera+ion
  • The Guest House
